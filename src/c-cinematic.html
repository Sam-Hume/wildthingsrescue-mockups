Vets across the county

Free at the vet

Drop a wild animal at any vet at no charge. If they prescribe medication, we cover it.

Our patients are wild, and that's the point. We keep handling to a minimum, we don't open for tours, and every decision is made with one goal: getting them back where they belong.

Rest

Quiet, dark and calm

Wild animals find people frightening. Rest in a calm space is often the best medicine we can give.

Treatment

Checked over, properly

Every new arrival gets a thorough health check, with vet support for X-rays, medication and more.

Nursery

Round-the-clock babies

Orphaned chicks, squabs and cygnets are hand-reared until they're ready to look after themselves.
