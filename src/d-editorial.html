Found an animal? Here's what happens next.

Most of our patients arrive because a member of the public stopped to help. This is how it works from your first call.

You call

Ring the casualty line

Call 01526 578579 or message us on Facebook. We're open 9am to 6pm, every day except Tuesday.

We advise

We talk you through it

We'll tell you how to keep the animal safe and what to do next. If we're closed, any 24-hour vet can help, free of charge.

We treat

Care and rehabilitation

With vets across the county, we handle everything from X-rays to medication, and we cover the cost of anything a vet prescribes.

They go home

Back to the wild

Once they're fit and ready, our patients are released. No tours, minimal handling, and wild instincts kept intact.
