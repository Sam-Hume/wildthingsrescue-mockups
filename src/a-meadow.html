Found an animal in trouble?

Don't panic. Here's what to do.

Most of our patients arrive because someone kind stopped to help. Here's how you can be that someone.

1

Keep it safe

If you can do so safely, pop the animal in a box with a towel and keep it somewhere quiet, dark and away from pets.

2

Give us a ring

Call our casualty line for advice, or message us on Facebook.

01526 578579

3

Closed? Try a vet

If we're closed, take it to your nearest 24-hour vet. You won't be charged, and if they prescribe medication, we pay.

We're open 9am to 6pm every day except Tuesday. We can't offer tours, as our patients are wild and need their rest.
